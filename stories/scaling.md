## Behind the experiments

A large run is too expensive to approach through trial and error alone.
Smaller experiments help us compare training recipes before committing the
larger computing budget.

Scaling laws estimate how results change with model size, training data and
compute. They guide decisions; they do not guarantee the outcome.

[Deriving Scaling Laws for OpenEuroLLM Models: Learning Rate, Batch Size and Loss](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.28308)
